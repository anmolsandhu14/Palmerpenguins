# Raw Data Dictionary

## Dataset Information
Source: palmerpenguins R package  
Original data collected by Dr. Kristen Gorman and the Palmer Station, Antarctica LTER.

Dimensions: 344 rows x 8 columns  
Note: Dataset contains missing values.

## Variables

- species  
  Penguin species (Adelie, Chinstrap, Gentoo)

- island  
  Island where the penguin was observed (Biscoe, Dream, Torgersen)

- bill_length_mm  
  Bill length in millimeters

- bill_depth_mm  
  Bill depth in millimeters

- flipper_length_mm  
  Flipper length in millimeters

- body_mass_g  
  Body mass in grams

- sex  
  Biological sex (male, female, missing)

- year  
  Year of observation (2007, 2008, 2009)
